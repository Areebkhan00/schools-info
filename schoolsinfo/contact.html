Frequently Asked Questions

How can I add my school to SchoolsInfo?

Please contact us via email at info@schoolsinfo.com with your school details, and our team will guide you through the registration process.

Is the information on SchoolsInfo verified?

Yes, we verify all information with schools directly. However, we recommend contacting schools directly for the most current information about admissions and facilities.

Can I download school information documents?

Yes, each school detail page has a download button where you can access detailed information documents in PDF or DOC format.

How do I search for schools with specific facilities?

Use our advanced search page where you can filter schools by location, library availability, canteen, boarding facilities, and sports amenities.
